Graphic Design with AI

Develop creative visual communication skills using professional graphic design tools and AI-powered creative workflows.

3 Months Duration Brand Systems Dual Certification Commercial AI Art

Course Overview

Our Graphic Design with AI programme is a hands-on creative course that equips you with the skills to produce stunning visual content for both print and digital media. You will learn the foundational principles of design — colour theory, typography, composition and layout — while mastering industry-standard tools like Adobe Photoshop and Adobe Illustrator through real-world projects.

This course goes beyond traditional graphic design by integrating Generative AI tools into the creative workflow. You will discover how to use AI for rapid concept generation, automated background removal, intelligent image enhancement, style transfer and AI-assisted branding — giving you the modern creative toolkit that agencies and brands demand today.
