UI/UX Design with Gen-AI & Agentic-AI

Master user experience, interface design, design thinking, prototyping and AI-powered design workflows to create meaningful digital products.

4 Months Duration Live Client Projects Dual Certification Gen-AI & Agentic AI

Course Overview

This comprehensive UI/UX Design programme is designed to transform you from a beginner into a skilled product designer capable of crafting intuitive, beautiful and user-centred digital experiences. You will learn the full spectrum of design — from foundational principles and user research to advanced prototyping and interface design using industry-standard tools like Figma.

What makes this course truly future-ready is its integration of Generative AI and Agentic AI workflows. You will discover how to leverage AI tools to accelerate ideation, automate repetitive design tasks, generate UI variations and conduct smarter usability analysis — preparing you for the next generation of design work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our UI/UX Design course? Find answers to the most common queries below.

No prior design experience is required. This course is structured to take you from the very basics of design thinking and visual principles all the way to advanced prototyping and AI-powered workflows. Whether you are a complete beginner or looking to transition into design, this programme is designed for you.

The course runs for 4 to 6 months depending on the batch schedule. Classes are held in both online and offline modes, with flexible timing options for weekday and weekend batches to accommodate working professionals and students alike.

You will gain hands-on experience with Figma — the industry-standard tool for interface design and prototyping. Additionally, you will explore a range of AI-powered design tools for generative design, automated layouts, image generation and intelligent user testing workflows.

Graduates are well-prepared for roles such as UI Designer, UX Designer, Product Designer, Interaction Designer and UX Researcher. The growing demand for designers who can integrate AI into their workflows makes our graduates highly competitive in both agency and in-house design positions.

The course includes dedicated modules on Generative AI for Designers and Agentic AI Workflows. You will learn to use AI tools for rapid prototyping, automated design variations, AI-assisted user research analysis and intelligent content generation — empowering you to work faster and smarter.
